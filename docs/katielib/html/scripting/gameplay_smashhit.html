Gameplay (Smash Hit)¶

knLevelHitSomething([player: integer])¶

Causes the player to crash and loose balls.

knLevelStreakAbort([player: integer])¶

Aborts the player’s streak “properly”, e.g. plays the sound in addition to dropping the streak.

knLevelStreakInc([player: integer])¶

Increments the player’s streak “properly”, playing sounds and giving any relevant achievements.

knLevelAddScore(score: integer, [player: integer])¶

Adds balls to the player “properly”, playing sounds and giving any relevant achievements.

Player Parameter¶

When functions take a player parameter, it can be one of the following values:

  • -1: The singleplayer player

  • 0: Player on the left

  • 1: Player on the right
