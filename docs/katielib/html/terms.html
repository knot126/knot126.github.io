Terms of Use¶

Thank you for wanting to use KatieLib! Please note that unlike KnShim, this mod requires permission to use and is subject to some simple, but hopefully easy to follow, terms for its use:

  1. Your mod must not use a generic package name and should be plainly identifiable

  2. Your mod must not be too similar to any other mod

  3. Your mod must include a “Powered by YipLoader” logo in the credits or when launching

We will not provide support for mods that violate these terms.
