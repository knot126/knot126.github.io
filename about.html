About my old username

My old username is from when I was signing up for a Fandom account, and I wanted a new username that would be hard to associate with my old accounts. As a result, I randomly chose the word "knot" and number 126 to make my new username "knot126". The word "knot" referred to a knot that one would tie with a rope, not anything else! I ended up liking the name, so it stuck, and it's what I identified with for a long time. Recently, I've tried moving away from that name since it's hard to be taken seriously in the furry community with it and I like the name kit (thanks KD!).
